FAQ

Questions People Ask
Before Starting

Is this for fat loss or muscle gain?
Both. On the intake form you'll tell me whether you want to cut, bulk, or recomp — and I'll set your calories, macros, and cardio specifically for that goal. Every plan is built around what YOU want to achieve.
Do I need a gym membership?
The cardio and training templates are built around general gym access. If you train at home, mention it on the form and I'll adjust accordingly.
Do I have to track macros?
I'll set your macros clearly. You can track them precisely if you want maximum accuracy, or follow the simplified version of your plan without logging every gram. Either way works — just don't freestyle entirely.
How is this different from a generic meal plan?
It's built from scratch based on your intake form — your food preferences, schedule, calorie needs, and specific goal. No template. No one-size-fits-all plan. It's designed for you to actually follow it.
Which plan should I choose?
If you want a quick focused cut or bulk — go 8 weeks. If you want a real transformation — go 12 weeks, it's the most popular for a reason. If you're going all in on maximum results — 20 weeks is for you.
How long until I receive my plan?
Within 24–48 hours of completing your intake form, your full custom meal plan will be delivered to your email with clear instructions on how to get started right away.
